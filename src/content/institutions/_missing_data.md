The field-by-field research completed on 2026-08-01 is now represented in each course record through structured `tuition`, `mec`, and `verification.estimatedFields` values. This file tracks only the remaining editorial backfill, so an estimate or not-applicable state does not live in two places.

|---|---|
| Undergraduate Computer Science | Course-specific URLs, duration, campus, admission, curriculum and official sources for UEL, UniCesumar, UniFil and UNOPAR/Anhanguera |
| Data Science and AI | Course-specific URLs, delivery details, admission, curriculum and official sources; preserve the existing new-course and estimated-workload states |
| Engineering and technical courses | Course-specific URLs, duration, campus and admission; confirm SENAI Londrina shifts and payment model before removing their estimate labels |
| Remaining design, infrastructure, marketing and postgraduate programs | Course-specific URLs, admission, curriculum and official sources; confirm every workload already labeled as estimated |

Research rules:

- Prefer official course pages, current curricula/PPCs, official admissions pages and MEC/INEP records.
- Record the actual review date and keep `verification.status: partial` while authoritative information is missing.
- Do not copy a generic institution URL into `officialUrl`.
- Do not publish an exact price without a dated, reliable source.
- Remove an item from this backlog only after the corresponding record carries the source and verification state.
